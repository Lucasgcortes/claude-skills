# Briefing

Ask in blocks of 3–4 questions, never all at once. Use AskUserQuestion for closed choices (channel, format, length, music mood).

## Block 1 · what and for whom
1. **Who watches?** Customers (all or a segment), prospects who don't know the brand, or the internal team (which area)?
2. Name of the feature/product.
3. **What problem existed before?** Ask for a real situation: what the person tried to do and where they got stuck (support tickets, community, sales calls).
4. What can they do now that they couldn't before?
5. What changes day to day or in results (time, sales, fewer errors, less support)?

If the answer is technical ("we added catalog caching"), rewrite it from the viewer's side and confirm ("your store opens faster and customers see products without waiting"). If there is no answer to question 3, say the issue is the feature's positioning, not the video, and suggest talking to product/support first.

Internal audiences can hear jargon (ROAS, CAC, churn). Customers usually can't.

**Say what it is, early.** The first headline names the product category in the viewer's words ("vale-refeição e alimentação", "conta PJ", "app de entregas"). Abstract words like "benefício" or "solução" make people guess.

**Pain → solution mirror.** The audience must recognize itself in the first seconds. Turn each pain into a concrete situation with an image (the spreadsheet at month end, colleagues' questions piling up, the invoice full of fees), then answer each pain with one solution scene in the same order. Abstract word lists ("Taxa de adesão · Recarga na planilha") don't create identification.

## Block 2 · format
- Channel and format: Reels/TikTok/Stories/Shorts (9:16), LinkedIn feed (1:1, 4:5 or 9:16), YouTube/site/presentation (16:9).
- Length: feature 20–45 s; brand/prospects 30–45 s; internal up to 60 s. LinkedIn feed: 20–40 s, assume muted autoplay (the story must work without sound).
- Highlights: max 3–4. If they list 6, propose the 4 strongest and say which were left out.
- CTA: where to access, link, handle, plan.
- Numbers: can they appear? Real or illustrative? Videos travel, so ask even for "internal" ones.

## Block 3 · files
- Full-page screenshots of each screen mentioned (or the page saved with SingleFile).
- Where the user finds the feature (menu path).
- Music (or mood: energetic, calm, neutral). No licensed track → `music_gen.py`.

## Jargon to translate (customer audiences)
| Avoid on screen | Say |
|---|---|
| API, integration, endpoint | "connects straight to…" / "data comes in by itself" |
| cache, optimization, performance | "opens faster" |
| deploy, release | "it's live" |
| backend, database | (leave out) |
| dashboard (for beginners) | "panel" |
| feature | "new" / "now you can" |
